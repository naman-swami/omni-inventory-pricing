# Retail Pricing & Inventory Science

## Amoroso-Robinson Pricing
For a constant-elasticity demand curve, optimal price maximizing profit:

$$P^* = \text{MC} \cdot \left( \frac{\varepsilon}{1 + \varepsilon} \right)$$

## Wilson Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)
$$\text{EOQ} = \sqrt{\frac{2 D S}{H}}$$
Where $D$ is annual unit demand, $S$ is fixed order cost, and $H$ is annual holding cost per unit.
